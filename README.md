# grok-bot-resources

Collection of Grok Bot templates, skills, architectures, and related GitHub projects from community sources.

Sourced from the X post by [@Greshamtrade](https://x.com/greshamtrade/status/2113769453007827036): "SpaceXAI just dropped 146 Grok Bot templates worth $29,200 for FREE" and the recommended 20 projects to open first.

## 20 GitHub Projects

01. **Agent Org Chart** — ready-made team structures for engineering, marketing, support, and sales. Pick the organization your job needs instead of inventing every role.  
   https://github.com/serenakeyitan/agent-org-chart

02. **Grok Factory** — one Firstmate routes software, research, and general work to the right specialists, then brings back reports or reviewed pull requests.  
   https://github.com/jaredtrichard/grok-factory

03. **multiBot** — creates purpose-built teammates that delegate heavy work to coding CLIs, keeping Grok Bot focused on coordination and returning the result.  
   https://github.com/simo255/multiBot

04. **Grok Bot Architecture** — a blueprint for a chief of staff, specialist bots, structured handoffs, connected tools, and human approval points on one shared computer.  
   https://github.com/tiagovilasboas/grok-bot-architecture

05. **Foreman** — inspects a hosted bot crew, surfaces stalled work, and records evidence behind completion claims. A separate checker supplies the passing verdict.  
   https://github.com/Archive228/foreman

06. **Grok Bot Second Brain** — a five-bot setup with Conductor, Capture, Memory, Ops, and Research, sharing a knowledge vault and defined responsibilities.  
   https://github.com/mKay00/grok-bot-second-brain

07. **grokbot-skills** — reusable skills for debugging, brainstorming, writing, and observing tasks, with upstream sources and licenses preserved.  
   https://github.com/jeremybrasher/grokbot-skills

08. **grokbot-x** — an X content workflow that scouts posts, prepares drafts, publishes through Typefully, and uses logged results to evaluate changes to its writing skills.  
   https://github.com/YannisKiefer/grokbot-x

09. **Restaurant Scout** — a specialist setup for finding food brands and creators, preparing shoppable-content scripts, and organizing the findings into a catalog workflow.  
   https://github.com/mykemueller1-ctrl/grok-bot-restaurant-scout

10. **Grok Bot Shopping** — shopping skills for product search, merchant checkout, Shopify discount-code tests, and order workflows, plus an optional Buyer bot.  
    https://github.com/steve228uk/grok-bot-shopping

11. **PhoneZero** — gives Grok Bot a phone workflow for tasks like restaurant bookings. It prepares the call plan, waits for approval, and reports the outcome.  
    https://github.com/function1st/PhoneZero

12. **Unlist** — a privacy assistant playbook for people-search opt-outs, with site records, letter generation, submission logs, and follow-up tracking.  
    https://github.com/shawnyeager/unlist

13. **Grok Bot Gateway** — lets coding agents and scripts list bots, read conversations, and send tasks through a gateway bot using a webhook routine.  
    https://github.com/dimpurr/grok-bot-gateway

14. **grokbot-queue** — queues and dispatches tasks across multiple Grok Bots, then collects their results using SSH and unofficial desktop automation.  
    https://github.com/ShuhangGe/grokbot-queue

15. **Thin Grok Bot** — a delegation skill that keeps the bot team focused on routing while deeper builds and investigations run through coding CLIs or cloud agents.  
    https://github.com/Luca-Blight/thin-grok-bot-deep-work-on-cli

16. **BOTROSTER** — a separate self-hosted alternative with persistent named teammates, shared files, a browser, scheduled work, and approval controls.  
    https://github.com/mandarwagh9/botroster

17. **Root Agent** — a coordinator that determines which specialists a request needs, manages assignments, and hands domain work to leads.  
    https://github.com/MrBekoX/root-agent-skill-framework

18. **Grok Bot Profiles** — four focused roles: Spec Writer, Bug Reproducer, PR Producer, and PR Verifier. Implementation and verification stay in separate conversations.  
    https://github.com/HAEGONG/grok-bot-profiles

19. **Grok Bot Templates** — 49 templates with defined jobs, outputs, skills, routines, and boundaries, plus team setups you can adapt to your work.  
    https://github.com/cobusgreyling/grok-bot-templates

20. **HyperGrok Trading Desk** — seven bots covering market data, research, strategy, risk, execution, and review. Starts in research mode; live trades require your approval.  
    https://github.com/galleonlabs/hypergrok-trading-desk

---

Give the first team a job with an obvious finish line: one researched brief, one reviewed PR, or one prepared batch of drafts.

Then check how much work you actually accepted and what it cost to produce. A larger roster earns its place when it improves that result.

The setup can be free. Paying several bots to repeat the same investigation still costs you.
